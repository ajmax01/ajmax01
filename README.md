# Hello! My name is Aidan Max, and I am a senior at Tufts University studying applied mathematics and quantitative economics. My passions are in statistical and financial modeling, and the projects featured here give a glimpse into what I've worked on. Sports data is a unique passion of mine as well, with several projects shown below.

## Featured Projects
### Python Program: Football (Soccer) Match Prediction Algorithm
Converted implied probabilities from historical betting odds into league-wide rating system using softmax function to generate rating differentials, optimized parameters/learning rates w/ scipy
### Price Modeling of Platinum and Palladium Futures
- Built a python program modeling Platinum & Palladium prices on stocks, commodities, economic indicators, alternative data using regression, correlation scanning, time series lagging
- Built multi factor model using Gold, $SPY, Crude Oil, Federal Funds Rate, Manufacturing CCI to proxy for macro hedging, risk sentiment, commodity cycles, monetary conditions, industrial demand
- Identified key relationships such as β=2.8 coefficient between Platinum and lagged military aircraft part prices
### Monte Carlo Simulation of Industrial Process Outcomes
- Built python program utilizing Monte Carlo Simulation to model cost savings & backlog improvement for theoretical processing success rates of platinum wire; used - Poisson distributed order flows, platinum interest costs, binomially distributed production success rates
- Projected $310,000 in annual savings and <12 annual backlogs by increasing success rates from 0.01 & 0.05 to 0.02 & 0.07 of 2 wire types; official business proposal for novel procedure submitted to company leadership
### Modeling Project: Monte Carlo Simulation in Comparison to the Black-Scholes Options Pricing Model
Coded Python simulation showing convergence of Monte-Carlo based models to Black Scholes
### Python Program: Stock Market Pairs Trading Algorithm
Utilized OLS, z score normalization trained on ~10 years of data to model spread; backtested and utilized walk forward testing to validate parameters and test for overfitting
### Research Paper: Effects of the Contract Year on Performance in NBA Players
Sourced data from multiple databases including Basketball Reference, Spotrac, and RAPTOR Index, tabulated data in Excel, cleaned data and ran final regressions in Stata
### Research Paper: Predictive Accuracy of NFL Betting Odds
Utilized data from multiple NFL seasons and ran regressions of odds accuracy on market size
